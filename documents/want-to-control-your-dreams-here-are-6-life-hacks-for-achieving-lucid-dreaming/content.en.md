## Reality check.

This method involves regularly checking reality. For example, constantly check if you are currently dreaming. Try to switch the light on and off, attempt to pierce your palm with your index finger to check if you feel pain. If you do this regularly in everyday life, this habit will carry over into your dreams, and you will become aware that you are inside a dream faster.

## Technology "Multiple Checks".

In a dream, to make sure that you are really asleep, try checking your surroundings several times. For example, repeat actions as if you are doing them twice. If something changes during the second check, it may indicate that you are in a dream.

## Intention. MILD Technique.

There are various methods and techniques developed to help you become aware of your dreams. For example, the 'MILD' technique (Mnemonic Induction of Lucid Dreams) involves repeating a phrase before sleep, such as 'I am aware that I am dreaming'. Such techniques can help your mind become more conscious during sleep.
