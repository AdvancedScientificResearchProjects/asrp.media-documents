# Innovating at BlockJam 2025: An Interview with Chang Cao

In this exclusive interview from BlockJam 2025, co-founder Chang Cao shares his perspective on the future of Theta technologies, the role of ASRP's dream data, and how hackathons can drive innovation at the intersection of AI, Web3, and collective intelligence. We discuss the vision behind these projects, the challenges of building meaningful applications, and what opportunities lie ahead for developers, researchers, and creators working with decentralized platforms.
