[**Siamon Piazolo**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/simon-piazolo/ "Siamon Piazolo")**:**

And afterwards, we also have an incubator program designed to help turn projects into actual startups, rather than just hackathon prototypes.

[**Siamon Piazolo**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/simon-piazolo/ "Siamon Piazolo")**:**

> Yeah, I think the main benefit is obviously the networking here at the hackathon — meeting other developers and seeing the new ideas growing within the Theta ecosystem. If they win, they get six months in the incubator, with funding and our support, so a hackathon project can really turn into a startup.
