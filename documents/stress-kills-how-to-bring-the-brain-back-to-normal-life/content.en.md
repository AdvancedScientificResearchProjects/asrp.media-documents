>

>
> *In 1975, R.A. Monroe proposed a method of brain stimulation using binaural rhythms, which  change the state of a person's psyche, in particular, from normal to drowsy, or increase concentration due to overexcitement. The mechanism of this effect is the response in the electrical EEG activity of the human brain at a frequency identical to the frequency of the binaural rhythm. This presence of a resonant response in the parietal lobes of the brain in response to acoustic stimulation has been recorded in many EEG studies. However, these methods contribute to an increase in the growth of excitation of the patient's brain electrical activity, which negatively affects their emotional state.*
>
> *We have proposed our own version of a method for rehabilitating emotional-affective disorders in a person using binaural rhythms - implemented by selecting rhythms whose frequency corresponds to the individual's own frequency characteristics of the brain and the typology of negative changes in the functional state of the person. The frequency selection is done manually, as  it is important to consider the actual state of the body  and its reaction  to the impact at the moment of sound selection (artificial intelligence has not yet coped with this task).*
>
> *To form rhythmic brain activity in the necessary direction for the first type of EEG pathology (absence of alpha rhythm registration), it is proposed to increase the power of the alpha rhythm in the occipital leads using binaural rhythms of sinusoidal shape with individually selected frequencies.*
>
> *The duration of exposure is 30 minutes, the number of sessions is determined by changes in the client's objective state and the degree of complexity of psycho-emotional pathologies. The number of sessions can range from 30 to 60 or more times.*
>

At the same time, not only the clinical status and psychophysiological indicators of clients improve, but also a pronounced positive mental well-being of the person arises.*

As a result, the typology of pathological changes in the functional state of the person is determined, and the relationship of these changes with psycho-emotional disorders is analyzed. To form rhythmic brain activity in the necessary direction, normal EEG activity is enhanced and pathological EEG activity is weakened. This method has no contraindications for use in conjunction with other rehabilitation methods.*
