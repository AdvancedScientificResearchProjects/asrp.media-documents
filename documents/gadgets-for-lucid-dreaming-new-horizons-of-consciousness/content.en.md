## Evolution of Dreams: Gadgets in Decryption Service

With the passage of time, the advancement of technology, and the deepening of research, it is expected that gadgets for lucid dream stimulation will become increasingly accessible and integrated into everyday life. They can be both personal devices and part of various sleep systems, providing the opportunity not only to experience thrilling adventures in the world of dreams but also to better understand one's own psychology.

## Modern technologies and lucid dreams: meet the gadgets.

In this article, we will explore various gadgets and technologies for lucid dreaming, based on research in the field of dreams, psychology, and brain activity. These innovations open up new horizons of consciousness and promise to change our perception of what dreams are and how we can interact with them.

## Lucid Dream Masks

Year: beginning of the 2000s.

History: The development of masks for lucid dream stimulation began in the 1980s, inspired by the works of LaBerge. (Read about LaBerge's ideas). Such masks use light or sound signals to 'awaken' consciousness in a dream. [1].

## 2. EEG Headbands

Year: 2010s.

History: Headsets with EEG sensors were developed in the 2010s. They are based on years of research on brain activity during sleep. The devices detect the REM sleep phase and use signals to stimulate lucid dreaming. [2]

## 3. Audio Stimulation Devices

Year: 2010s

History: Audio stimulation devices began to be developed in the 2000s, based on research in the field of the impact of sounds on dream content. These devices use sound signals to activate lucid dreaming. [3]

## 4. Mobile Apps (Dream Mobile Applications)

Year: 2010s.

History: Mobile applications for recording and analyzing dreams began to be developed in the 2010s. They are based on research in the field of dream psychology and lucidity. With the development of mobile platforms, dream recording applications were created. Users can keep electronic dream journals, which can help improve their lucidity in sleep. [4]

## 5. Sleep Trackers

Year: 2010s.

History: The development of sleep trackers began in the 2000s, based on research studying physiological changes during sleep. Such devices detect sleep phases and moments for lucid dream stimulation.

## 6. Smart Sleep Masks

Year: 2010s.

History: Smart sleep masks began to be developed in the 2010s, based on research showing that visual and audio signals can influence dream content and promote lucid dreaming. [6]

## 7. Virtual Reality

Year: 2010s.

History: The use of virtual reality for lucid dream stimulation began in the 2010s. The technology utilizes visual and auditory signals to stimulate lucid dreams. [7]

## 8. Dream Manipulation Devices

Year: 2010s.

History: The development of devices for manipulating dreams began in the 2010s, based on research showing that consciousness in dreams can be influenced. The devices use signals to activate specific scenarios in dreams. [8]

## 9. Telegram Dream Bot

Year: 2023.

History: In 2023, ASRP released the Arcanum12thBot telegram bot for lucid dreaming, developed by Mikhail Kapustin based on the research of Denis Banchenko. (You can read about the research in the articles: "Banchenko Algorithm: a method for stimulating lucid dreaming that has a positive impact on health.") The bot functions as a dream journal, analyzing and deciphering dreams, as well as finding similarities between users' dreams and keeping statistics of such matches.
