### ASRP.media:

[**Alexandra Bernadotte**](https://all-andorra.com/ru/aleksandra-bernadott-ii-v-medicine-eto-assistivnaya-texnologiya-no-otvetstvennost-nesyot-chelovek/) shared her opinion on this issue. According to her, despite the elegance of the solution, its implementation is possible with certain caveats.

### Alexandra Bernadotte:

> *First, to preserve data on brain reactions to stimuli, we need labeled data. That is, we need precisely labeled reactions to specific stimuli. The more precise and identical the conditions, the better the result.*
>
> *Second, we move to a new type of testing that can be learned. If there is a labeling system and it is known that people react differently to certain jokes, these jokes need to be repeated multiple times in each interview. As a result, testing loses spontaneity and a moment of cheating arises — candidates can learn the correct reactions. Labeled data allows us to know exactly how to react, and this can be learned.*
>
> *For example, the methodology of the project **HyperCortex**, aimed at neuroplasticity, allows learning certain brain patterns. If we have labeled data, we can learn these patterns.*
>
> *On the one hand, this is possible, but on the other hand, to create verifiable and falsifiable information about a person, the most complete labeling is necessary, including external conditions, such as whether it is a sunny day or cloudy. We understand that the reaction of a group of people to the same joke can change depending on the conditions, and all these parameters must be taken into account in the dataset*.

Патент называется иначе, но в кулуарах я называю это направленной нейропластичностью. Суть в том, что мы можем индуцировать появление новых синапсов или даже нейронов в конкретных областях мозга, что по сути является нейрогенезом. Конечно, это долгий процесс. Но в контексте послеродовой депрессии одна из теорий указывает на необратимую потерю нейронов, что делает это состояние потенциально некурабельным*, - делится мнением исследователь.
