# ASRP.art: Awakening art or how a dream becomes the soul of the canvas / ASRP.art: пробуждение искусства или как сон становится душой холста

## EN

Art has always been a reflection of the inner world of a person, his thoughts, emotions, and subconscious impulses. Today, in the era of digital technologies and global changes, it is reaching a new level, where the boundaries between dream and reality begin to blur. We live in times when the creative process is no longer limited to the artist's workshop - it becomes a bridge between consciousness and technology.

Contemporary artists and researchers turn to transcendental states, such as lucid dreams, hypnagogia, and meditation, to draw inspiration and create works that reflect not only their vision but also deep inner experiences. These works are already becoming objects of admiration in the art market, where the objective assessment of their impact on the viewer becomes as important as aesthetics.

The fusion of art and technology opens new horizons: with the help of neurointerfaces and consciousness analysis, we can capture the power of the impact of works of art, turning them into unique substrates of mental states. This concept is presented in the project [**ASRP.art**](https://asrp.tech/ru/projects/asrp.art "ASRP.art is an innovative artistic and scientific project exploring the intersection of dreams, consciousness, and creativity. ### Valeria Ovsyannikova:

> *Thank you for this question. This is a concept based on the connection between perception, emotions, and neurophysiology. When a person interacts with a work of art, their brain responds to visual stimuli, triggering associations and emotions that can alter the state of consciousness (**[For example, this is supported by research conducted on a group of gamers at one of the computer game championships, which showed that the brain rhythms of team members synchronized in such a way that they acted as a single organism, uniting their states through visual images.](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0028393222001750?via%3Dihub "Scientists from the University of Helsinki found that the brain waves of two players playing together online synchronized - even if they were in different rooms. The study was published in the journal Neuropsychologia. When two people communicate in the real world, they experience neural synchronization. Scientists believe that this helps facilitate empathy and cooperation during face-to-face communication. It turned out that a similar process occurs during online gaming as well. Psychologists analyzed the brain waves of several pairs of people as they played a racing game together. The participants were in separate soundproof rooms. This experiment also showed that synchronization in the alpha and gamma frequency bands leads to improved performance in the game. The authors of the study believe that social online interactions can activate fundamental mechanisms of empathy, which may lead to improved social relationships in the real world beyond games.")**). >
> *To enhance this effect, I integrate modern technologies such as neurointerfaces. We record the brain activity of viewers to analyze their emotional and cognitive reactions to my works. This creates a kind of closed loop: the artwork influences the viewer, the viewer influences the artwork through their reactions, and this, in turn, amplifies the effect of impact. This process resembles the effect of quantum entanglement: an invisible connection is formed between the author and the viewer, through which not only information is transmitted, but also the very state of consciousness.*
>
> *In my works, I strive to convey not only sensations but also entire states of consciousness that arise at the boundary between sleep and reality, between the internal and the external. These are spaces where the quantum nature of the mind manifests most clearly, and the boundary between the observer and the observed blurs, turning the artwork into a mediator of experience.*
