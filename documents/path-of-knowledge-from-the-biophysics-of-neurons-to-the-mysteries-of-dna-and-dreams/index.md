During my studies, specialized subjects began only in the third year, and it was actually then that I started to achieve something quite serious in my studies. >

>
> I wrote the specialist diploma already in another department, which turned out to be very close and dear to me. We worked with peripheral neurons and, in fact, obtained very interesting results. I worked with wonderful experimenters; this is experimental biophysics, which had a huge impact on me.
>
> Accordingly, at the same time, I was engaged in my own research related to the body, the biology of the body, the physiology of the body, the functional activities of the body. I read books related to science, experiments, trains of thought in general, that is, with the philosophy of science.

>

And since 2018, I worked with a rather interesting, in its own way outstanding scientist, rather under his guidance, Maxim Rempel. >

And on the other hand, I find the modern scientific system, which, I repeat, is structured in such a way that movement is practically impossible, to be quite unappealing and pathetically poor, as the Greek sophist Zeno said.
